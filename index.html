What We Do

Our Lawn Care Services

Everything your yard needs — handled by people who take pride in their craft.

🌿

Lawn Mowing

Clean, even cuts every time. We mow at the right height for your grass type, leaving your lawn looking sharp and healthy all season long.

📐

Lawn Edging

Crisp, defined borders along sidewalks, driveways, and garden beds. Edging is the detail that makes a good-looking yard look great.

🔧

Weed Whacking & Trimming

We get the spots your mower can't — around fences, trees, posts, and tight corners. No overgrown edges left behind.

🌱

Weed Pulling

Manual weed removal from garden beds, borders, and lawn areas. We pull them out by the root so they don't come back fast.

🪵

Mulch Installation

Fresh mulch beds that look amazing and help your plants thrive. We prep the area, lay an even bed, and clean up when we're done.

🌸

Flower & Plant Planting

Brighten up your yard with seasonal flowers and small plants. We'll plant them properly so they root well and bloom beautifully.

🍂

Yard Cleanup

Seasonal cleanups to clear out leaves, debris, dead plants, and winter mess. Spring and fall cleanups available — we haul it all away.

🏡

General Lawn Maintenance

Regular scheduled maintenance to keep your property looking its best all season. Weekly, biweekly, or custom schedules available.
